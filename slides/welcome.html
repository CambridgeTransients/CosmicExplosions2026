Goals of the Week

01 — Primary Build connections Bridge observers, modellers, and software developers across GRBs, kilonovae, supernovae, TDEs, and LFBOTs.
02 Identify open problems Surface the real blockers — in modelling, inference, multi-messenger follow-up, and data access — and be honest about where we're stuck.
03 Community infrastructure Make concrete progress on data standards and software interoperability — what do we need to converge on before Rubin changes everything?
04 Chart the path forward What does the ET / ELT / WST / UVEX era demand of us now? Leave with action items, not just ideas.

Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive workshop for everyone.

  • Be respectful and inclusive. Treat all participants with courtesy regardless of career stage, background, or identity.
  • Critique ideas, not people. Robust scientific debate is welcome; personal attacks are not.
  • Make space for everyone. Be mindful of how much you speak — invite others in, especially in discussions.
  • No harassment of any kind. This includes all forms of discrimination, intimidation, and unwanted behaviour.
  • Chatham House Rule. Unpublished results and ideas shared in discussions stay in the room unless the speaker explicitly agrees otherwise.

Concerns can be raised with Nikhil Sarin at any point during the week. Full policy: cambridgetransients.github.io/CosmicExplosions2026/coc.html
