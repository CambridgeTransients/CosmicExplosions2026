Code of Conduct

27–31 July 2026 · Ryle Room, KICC, Cambridge

CosmicExplosions 2026 is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for all participants, regardless of career stage, background, gender, ethnicity, nationality, or any other characteristic. All attendees are expected to uphold the following principles throughout the workshop.

Be respectful and inclusive

Treat all participants with courtesy and respect. This applies equally in talks, discussions, hallway conversations, and social events. Differences in career stage, methodology, or scientific viewpoint are not grounds for dismissiveness.

Critique ideas, not people

Robust scientific debate is not only welcome but encouraged. Challenge arguments with evidence and reason. Personal attacks, condescension, or dismissive language directed at individuals are not acceptable.

Make space for everyone

Be mindful of how much space you take in discussions. Actively invite contributions from others, especially early-career participants. If you notice someone struggling to contribute, help open the floor to them.

No harassment of any kind

Harassment includes but is not limited to: discriminatory comments, intimidation, unwanted physical contact, sustained disruption of talks or discussions, and inappropriate sexual attention. Such behaviour will not be tolerated and may result in removal from the workshop.

Chatham House Rule

This workshop involves discussion of ongoing and unpublished work. Unless a speaker explicitly agrees otherwise, results and ideas shared in sessions should not be attributed to individuals or shared publicly. Participants are free to use the information they hear, but not to reveal who said it or where it was discussed.

💬
Reporting concerns
If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code of conduct, please speak to the organiser Nikhil Sarin directly or by email at nsarin.astro@gmail.com. All reports will be treated with discretion and taken seriously.
